P4HOW TO BUILD IT (WHY THE LIVE CARD BREAKS)
What's broken: the front face is positioned absolutely, and the photo face is in normal flow at the image's own height. So the card grows to about 480px of empty space, and the title, address and Photo pill stack on top of each other.
Fix: make the flipper display:grid, and put both faces in grid-area:1/1. The grid row takes the height of the front's content. The back is position:relative, and its <img> is position:absolute; inset:0; width:100%; height:100%; object-fit:cover, so the image never sets the height.
3D: the wrapper has perspective:1400px. The flipper has transform-style:preserve-3d and a 600ms cubic-bezier(.3,.7,.3,1) transition. Both faces have backface-visibility:hidden, and the back has rotateY(180deg). Toggle the flipper between rotateY(0) and rotateY(180deg).
Reduced motion and access: with prefers-reduced-motion, crossfade over 150ms instead of flipping. The face that isn't showing gets inert and aria-hidden. Move focus to "↻ Details" after a flip, and back to "↻ Photo" when flipping back.
Test: the Adams Family Haunt and Glens Terror Drive cards are the same height as their neighbors, give or take their own rows, with no overlap, at 400px and at 320px. On mobile, use the same rules.
