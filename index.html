About

About Hauntfinder

Hauntfinder is a free community guide to Halloween displays, home haunts, trick-or-treat stops, and local events.

The site started in the Mount Olive area of New Jersey and has expanded to help people find Halloween activities in surrounding communities. Visitors can search by ZIP code, choose a distance, review visiting details, and plan their stops.

Have a decorated home, home haunt, treat stop, or Halloween event? You can submit a listing for free.

Hauntfinder is a community resource, not a contest. Listings are reviewed before appearing on the site, and hosts can submit updates when details change.

Created and maintained by a local resident.

Meet Skully

Skully is our family's 12-foot skeleton and the face of several community activities at our home in Budd Lake, New Jersey.

What started with Halloween decorations has grown into something we enjoy sharing with neighbors throughout the year.

Our family has hosted a free pumpkin patch, lemonade fundraisers, and offered refreshments during summer power outages. We enjoy finding simple ways to bring people together and help when we can.

Halloween at the Adams Family Haunt

Halloween is one of our favorite times of year.

We transform our property into a walk-around Halloween experience featuring decorated scenes, photo opportunities, and seasonal surprises.

Our family has participated in local Trunk or Treat events, earning awards three years in a row, and our home display has received Spook the House recognition twice.

We love seeing families visit, take pictures, and share their experiences.

For current dates, hours, and visiting details, check the Adams Family Haunt listing on Hauntfinder.

Skully's Costume Closet

Skully's Costume Closet is our free community costume program, now in its third year.

We provide gently used costumes and accessories for infants, children, teens, and adults. The goal is simple: help more people participate in Halloween without worrying about the cost of a costume.

Inventory changes frequently, and we cannot confirm every costume or size in advance. Visitors are welcome to stop by during posted hours to see what's available.

Costume donations are accepted year-round.

For current hours and other details, check Skully's Costume Closet under Events on Hauntfinder.
