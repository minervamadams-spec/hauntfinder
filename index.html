About Skully

Meet Skully, the Adams family’s 12-foot skeleton and community mascot at 5 N Mount Olive Road, Budd Lake, NJ. Skully brings neighbors together throughout the year, with plenty of fun and a helping hand when it’s needed.

Over the years, our family has offered a free community pumpkin patch, hosted lemonade fundraisers, and provided free beverages during summer electricity outages. Follow Skully to see what’s happening next.

Halloween with the Adams family

Halloween is our busiest season. Alongside our home haunt, we participate in Trunk or Treat, winning three years in a row, and have won Spook the House two years in a row. We love seeing visitors’ photos and being tagged in your posts.

Skully’s Costume Closet · year three

Now in its third year, our community costume closet offers free costumes and accessories for infants, children, and adults. The closet is in the garage behind our home. Take what you need, give what you can, and check the listings and social updates for current hours.

Donations welcome year-round

Costumes and accessories are welcome all year. Leave donations on the front porch, or contact us to coordinate a drop-off. Your donations help more neighbors enjoy dressing up.
