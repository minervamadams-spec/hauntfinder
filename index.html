Skully’s Costume Closet

Halloween should be fun for everyone. The Adams family’s community costume closet offers free costumes and accessories for children and adults at 5 N Mount Olive Road, Budd Lake, NJ. Take what you need, give what you can.

Donations welcome year-round

Have costumes or accessories ready for their next home? Donations help keep the closet stocked for the community. Leave them on the front porch, or email Minerva to coordinate a drop-off.

Skully’s Closet sits in the garage behind the house. Follow Skully for current hours, seasonal updates and community events. We love seeing your costume photos and being tagged in your posts.
